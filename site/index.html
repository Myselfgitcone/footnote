      How it works

      1. Each morning a Lambda fetches new 10-Ks from SEC EDGAR into S3 and splits them into sections: Business, Risk Factors, MD&A, Legal Proceedings, Cybersecurity, Market Risk.
      2. A second Lambda cuts the sections into passages, embeds them with bge-small on CPU, and stores them in Pinecone. Headline figures come from the XBRL data filed with each 10-K.
      3. Your question goes to a LangGraph pipeline. Claude reads it and picks the companies, years, figures and what to search for. Text search and figure lookup run in parallel. Then Claude writes an answer that may cite only what was found. Every run is traced in LangSmith.

      Covers fiscal 2021 onward for Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, NVIDIA, Meta, Tesla, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Goldman Sachs, Walmart, Costco, Target, Johnson & Johnson, UnitedHealth, Pfizer, Exxon Mobil, Chevron, Coca-Cola and Disney. Not investment advice.
